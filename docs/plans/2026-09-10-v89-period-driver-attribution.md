without changing the live portfolio, original replay or running full reporter.

contributions separately. It does not yet allocate each trade's marked value,
fees and funding across the original split. Three XYZ positions cross that
boundary in the complete full-fee control account; exit-date buckets are not an
equivalent measure. The raw account archive owns settlements and balances;
the exact boundary event owns contemporaneous marks. Existing authenticated
reports and receipts own the case/window/source identities.

1. Add report-only typed attribution for earlier, later and full contributions.
   Boundary marks and funding close the earlier period; boundary entries and

   nonpositive equity. Reconcile every gross, fee, funding and net component to

2. Start with hand-calculated synthetic 2030 fixtures: long/short carried trades,
   entries/exits exactly at the split, terminal open exposure, funding clocks,
   empty and negative accounts, duplicate/unknown identities, missing marks,
   altered snapshots and sealed/non-original historical contracts.
3. Freeze the tested implementation before evaluating original historical data.
   Bind the original receipts and qualified reports, extract the exact split

   every consumed artifact and preserve source/case/clock identity.
4. Reconcile all six period totals. Decompose gross movement, fees and funding by
   asset and direction; normalize comparisons by 28 and 20 calendar days.
   Diagnose concentration and carried exposure separately from trade frequency.
   Any unexplained residual prevents an accepted diagnostic conclusion.
5. Record the analysis and its limits in the research journal and build one
   portable HTML report for the portfolio owner. This is the metric-diagnostics
   workflow with the build-report executive specification. Required report roles:
   title; Executive Summary; findings with visual evidence; next steps; open
   questions; assumptions/caveats. Preserve exact scripts, sources, grouping and
   visual decisions in supporting metadata. Do not publish externally.

High confidence requires source authentication, synthetic checks and exact
component reconciliation. The result is descriptive historical attribution, not
causal proof of a market regime, a newly unseen holdout, a new selection rule,
or a live recommendation. Different histories or fills can alter the result.
Continue all 48 original cases and the unchanged full-report handoff. Both
