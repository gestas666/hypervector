# Quarter-budget acquire-to-begin accounting probe

Research only. Preserve the active backend, TUI, five frozen collectors, long
native comparisons and running exact-health qualification studies.

The qualified bridge persists request obligations at `CollectorAttempts.begin`.
The original quarter-v2 collector first reserves weight in `RequestBudget.acquire`,
then invokes capture and the recording session. Its original restart restores
indexed request weights plus the bridge's durable attempt obligations. A death
between the in-memory acquisition and durable begin may therefore lose that
reservation. This is a source-supported hypothesis, not yet an observed failure.

Run both exact frozen quarter-v2 source versions in private new namespaces under
OS network denial. Observe original function call/return events, without replacing
their code or clocks. Suspend at three declared boundaries: before acquire,
successful acquire return, and durable begin return. Retain exact local budget
state, then SIGKILL only the owned experimental child and resume it in a new
interpreter using the existing startup/recovery path.

Measure original in-memory reserved weight before death, durable attempts and
planned weight, raw/index counts, service submissions/transports, recovered local
reservations, and original recovery return/fault. The durable-begin control must
retain its unresolved obligation; a pre-begin case must not invent an HTTP return,
raw response, service transport or usable observation. Authenticate frozen source
imports and both source versions' applicable request-budget implementations.

Success for this probe is an attributable boundary classification, not a repair
claim. If a successful original acquisition disappears from durable accounting,
record the exact no-dispatch scope and design a successor preserving that local
obligation without representing it as venue traffic. Compare leaving the
limitation explicit with adding a durable reservation and a one-to-one link to
the later attempt. Do not alter live budget policy or claim observed capital loss.

Unproven until tested: boundary reachability, genuine process-death behavior,
complete recovery classification and distinction between no transport and an
unresolved transport. This probe does not qualify power loss, whole-service
restart, full rolling-window capacity or economic improvement.
