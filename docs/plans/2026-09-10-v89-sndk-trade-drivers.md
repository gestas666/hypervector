# Original SNDK trade driver diagnostic

Research only. Freeze this scope before evaluating trade-level differences.
Use original cases 0/1, 4/5 and 8/9, first core observations, full-prefix XYZ
history and recorded exit clock, July 21 through September 7 UTC. Long trades
are the primary investigation selected by the completed period attribution;
short trades remain context. No prospective outcomes or live state are read.

Question: is later SNDK weakness primarily fewer large winners, different exits,
or a broad decline across its observed entry conditions? This is descriptive
selection after seeing period results, not a new independent hypothesis test.

Reuse authenticated original trade reports and the qualified marked-period
attribution. Bind exact provider publications at accepted entry timestamps and
the native 15-minute price source declared in the original case manifest. Do
not rerun the provider, account engine or original 48-case coordinator.

Retain every SNDK trade in each fixed case. Report full-lifecycle outcomes by
entry cohort (before/at-or-after August 18), direction and exit reason. Keep
marked earlier/later contribution separate, particularly the carried long.
Measure count, win/loss counts, gross/net cash, gross return per notional,
holding duration and dependence on the three largest winners. No trade or
strategy is excluded because of an unfavorable result.

Preserve causal entry ATR, channel boundaries, reference price and stop anchor
from the exact accepted provider publication. Calculate ATR/reference and
breakout/channel-width ratios, with no fitted threshold or new filter. Record
UTC entry hour without asserting a market-session label.

For exit context, use only native 15-minute bars entirely inside each modeled
holding interval. Report favorable/adverse completed-close excursions and the
signed gap from the best completed close to actual modeled gross exit PnL.
These are sampled close paths, not continuous extrema, venue marks or executable
peak exits. Report coverage, not zero-filled missing paths. First-hour returns
are available only if the trade remains open through that hour; show censoring.
Instantaneous recorded-clock exits have no complete held bar and stay explicit.

Tests must establish long/short signs, exact boundaries, empty/instant paths,
missing/duplicate bars, finite/positive prices, duplicate/unmatched provider
identities, carried-period separation and concentration removals. Independently
check aggregate cash against the qualified reports, original raw closed accounts
and marked attribution; preserve all artifacts and source hashes. Freeze tested
operator source before financial evaluation. Use the earlier faster-entry and
ambiguous-stop studies as prior art; do not repeat them without new evidence.

High confidence requires exact source identity, valid temporal coverage and
independent arithmetic reconciliation. Different fills, unsampled intrabar
movement or selection bias can change interpretation. A causal explanation,
new policy and prospective profitability remain unproven. The next action must
follow the measured result, with no live parameter or deployment change.
