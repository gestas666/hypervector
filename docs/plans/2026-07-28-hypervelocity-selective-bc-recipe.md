# Hypervelocity Selective BTC B/C Source-Parity Plan

**Goal:** Reproduce the updated Hypertrader BTC Recipe B/C baseline from pinned
source, then determine whether it survives causal, venue-matched execution. No
result in this plan changes runtime authority automatically.

**Pinned upstream:** `hpombo1337/hypertrader` commit
`ad15f2bdc949619be495a5f516babd08f7f52a90`.

## Known facts

- Capital structure labels come from the confirmed 5-minute classifier.
- Recipe B is BTC long on a confirmed `BREAKOUT/BK_UPWARD` onset during
  00:00-08:00 UTC with rule-agreement confidence at least 0.6. Maximum hold is
  48 five-minute bars.
- Recipe C is BTC long fading a confirmed `TREND_DOWN` onset during
  07:00-16:00 UTC. Maximum hold is 288 five-minute bars.
- Both use the first close reaching 20 price bps or 130 BTC points, whichever
  occurs first, with no stop in the source experiment.
- Source headline evidence is 15/15 B plus 11/11 C during a July search. It is
  explicitly in-sample and awaiting forward validation.
- The source backtest uses Binance-aligned closes, subtracts a fixed 8 bps,
  tests close-to-close target hits, and closes each simulated trade before
  evaluating the next onset. It therefore does not represent a chronological,
  one-position, leveraged portfolio.
- The previously generated Hypervector finite-proxy alignment report is
  retained as negative evidence. It did not implement the now-available source
  classifier and must not be used as a source-parity result.

## Frozen classifier baseline

- 5-minute bars, 600-bar rolling history.
- EMA 20/50/200, ADX 14, ATR 14, RSI 14, Bollinger Bands 20x2, volume SMA 20.
- Rolling 24-hour percentile ranks: 288 five-minute bars.
- Strict breakout: frozen 48-bar range, 3 consecutive closes beyond the range,
  2 bps clearance, and volume ratio at least 2.0.
- State machine: two raw breakout confirmations, ten-bar breakout TTL,
  fifteen-bar cooldown; ordinary states require three confirmations and a
  five-bar minimum duration.
- A label timestamp is the bar-open timestamp. The decision is not causally
  available until that five-minute bar closes.

## Experiment contract

- Hyperliquid BTC candles, books, public trades, mark/oracle prices, and funding.
- Maker entry with public trade-through evidence and a 300-second TTL.
- One BTC position, 70% isolated margin, 40x leverage, 8% gross-margin target.
- Authentic fee, funding, mark-price liquidation, partial liquidation, and
  chronological equity accounting.
- Recipe-specific maximum holds; no stop in the source-parity baseline.
- Directional LightGBM stays annotation-only. It may be evaluated as a separate
  challenger but cannot filter or size the baseline.
- Trial outcomes are reported, not used as implicit execution abort gates.

## Work sequence

1. Preserve the upstream revision, source hashes, recipe definitions, and known
   methodological gaps.
2. Generate fresh 5-minute labels by executing the pinned upstream classifier
   against uninterrupted Hyperliquid 1-minute candles resampled causally.
3. Add source-parity tests for breakout persistence, state confirmation, session
   boundaries, onset extraction, and decision availability at bar close.
4. Reproduce the source close-only score on Hyperliquid candles as a diagnostic,
   including overlapping-trade disclosure.
5. Replay B, C, and chronological combined B/C through the existing maker replay
   with the frozen portfolio contract.
6. Compare only pre-registered challengers, one factor at a time: source timing
   versus causal +5-minute timing; close-only versus intrabar/orderbook; source
   session filters; optional extension cap; annotation-only ML ranking.
7. Run focused tests, the non-network Python suite, Rust tests, Ruff, formatting,
   file-size checks, and artifact accounting.

## Success criteria and falsifiers

- **Source parity:** deterministic fresh labels match a fixture generated by the
  pinned source. A mismatch falsifies the adapter.
- **Signal quality:** positive net expectancy with stable chronological slices;
  all entries, timeouts, and liquidations remain visible. Negative expectancy or
  concentration in one slice falsifies the candidate on this sample.
- **Execution feasibility:** maker-fill and target accounting reconcile to
  orderbook/trade evidence. Missing or contradictory venue evidence invalidates
  the affected observation rather than creating a fallback fill.
- **Promotion:** not part of this plan. A later frozen forward shadow window is
  required before changing runtime authority.

## Risks and unknowns

- The 26/26 selection may be severe multiple-testing overfit.
- Source timestamps are bar opens while source entries use that bar's close;
  applying the signal at the timestamp would leak up to five minutes.
- Binance research closes can disagree with Hyperliquid tradeability and path.
- A 40x, 70%-margin, no-stop position may liquidate before a later close target.
- Local Hypertrader label storage was produced by a different running revision:
  fresh pinned-code replay matched 757/852 primary labels (88.8%) over the
  inspected overlap. It is not accepted as a parity oracle.

## Boundary

This is report-only research. No commit, push, runtime promotion, live order
construction, model authority change, or secret disclosure is authorized by
this plan.
