# Captured account authentication and case metrics

The research reporting path now accepts a complete captured account on its original native clock schedule. It authenticates the caller-pinned report, the four required artifacts, the complete source calendar, original source references, config, contract and expected comparison cell. Actual captured studies are rebound to their authenticated registration and their real outcome seals are checked before outcomes are opened. Synthetic controls are explicitly dated 2030 or later. The candidate is research-only; it does not authorize or install a live change.

The historical report interface cannot safely be reused by changing a label: it has a three-artifact format, historical scope and a minute/late-exit schedule that omits the captured funding offsets. The new case reader independently recalculates account postings in Decimal and preserves those offsets in equity, drawdown, duration-weighted exposure and every entry disposition. Historical entrypoints retain their existing scope and schedule checks; shared arithmetic is extracted behind those checks.

Fault injection reproduced a further verification gap in the candidate reader. Changing or deleting funding inside a retained source event passed while its committed posting and source-calendar value remained unchanged. The reader now requires the source event, committed event and source calendar to agree exactly. The retained red run has two expected failures and nineteen passing controls. The subsequent reader/reconciliation run passed thirty tests, including the complete two-day synthetic account. This is a research-artifact defect, not evidence of an incorrect live funding charge.

The case report provides full/earlier/later/daily equity paths, costs, closed and terminal-open contributions, accepted/rejected/route-excluded proposals, all seven asset groups, strategy and direction groups, and exposure/contribution concentration.
