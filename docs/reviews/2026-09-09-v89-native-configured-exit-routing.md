## Limits and next integration

This is not a replay of the full native poll transport. Historical source-age
withholding receipts are absent from these comparison payloads; preservation is
covered by controlled tests only. No independent canonical history is supplied
here. Controlled economic invariance holds for one intent per asset and seven
available slots; opposing-intent and reduced-capacity cases demonstrate why it
cannot be generalized.

The tested ages do not qualify HYPE's soft-extension interval or future UNKNOWN
handling. All frozen profit-lock policies are `none`; active profit-lock policy
behavior is not qualified by these historical cycles. Complete native controller
journal payloads are not retained in cycle rows, only counts; full action fields
and exit scopes are retained. No dynamic account integration, fills, executable
quotes, costs, funding, PnL or prospective outcomes are inferred.
