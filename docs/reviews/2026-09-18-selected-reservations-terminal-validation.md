# Selected durable reservations: completed simultaneous studies

Both independently executed 1,300-second studies passed: candidate source in
1,339.31 seconds and its exact archive in 1,337.21 seconds, including staging
and verification. Both used all five original collector interpreters, real
wall clocks, synthetic full-day responses, two-second first-response latency,
the delayed peer-429 scenario and explicit reservation version 1. Network
access was denied. This validates research integration, not a trading return.

The independent readers were rerun after completion and reproduced both saved
reports exactly. Each study exercised all six native history slots, six
backfills and at least three quarter-collector cycles. Minimum native spacing
was 60,024 ms and 60,020 ms. Each native guard observed four peer-429 refusals
and subsequently admitted traffic. Ordered consumed peer-health bytes and
raw-derived observation grids reconciled. Total original attempts were 571
and 567; unique shared transports were 140 and 134. These are distinct counts.

Each applicable quarter-v2 collector retained 234 reservations/bindings/begins
in the candidate study and 232 in the archive study. Corresponding cumulative
local weights were 5,438 and 5,397. No unbound local obligation or binding
without a begin remained. Rolling expiry was exercised; the sustained-only
verifier coverage's remaining line is the unbound-record branch, exercised by
the separate corruption/boundary suite. Both frozen source trees still match
all 5,807 manifest files: 11,614 matches, zero changed or missing. Source
archive SHA-256 remains
`738c58354f6516938ca71642fb625c2dc1673b3452fcd204d4efcd7b02a18958`.

A separate recovery experiment found a real integration defect in this frozen
version. After successful five-second runs, all five fresh stopped-resume
processes failed in both candidate and archived source. Four rejected their
existing observation-history files; the native process attempted to recreate
existing peer links. Logical market rows and service request counts were
unchanged. This does not invalidate the sustained collection result, but it
precludes claiming restart readiness. A separate successor is under test.

Two older extracted source trees were removed only after their retained,
journal-pinned compressed archives and every tracked file were verified,
with zero direct extraction pins or open handles. Recovery commands and hashes
remain in the new resume study's extraction-compaction evidence. Measured free
space changed from 8,528,625,664 to 8,995,844,096 bytes; concurrent activity
prevents treating that difference as an exact reclaimed-byte measurement.

Recommendation: retain the selected-reservation candidate as validated for
these sustained offline scenarios, and prioritize restart recovery before any
deployment proposal. High confidence in the measured scope. Whole-service
SIGKILL, orphan recovery, power loss, external IP headroom, production overhead
and operational migration/rollback remain unqualified. Lost/doubled obligations,
changed original scheduling, an unbacked HTTP begin or contradictory source
replay would falsify the associated integration claim. Live strategy, universe,
leverage, sizing and deployment remain unchanged; no live approval is requested.
