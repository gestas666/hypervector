## Decision and remaining work

**Recommendation / why now:** retain this isolated writer and source validation
as infrastructure for the predeclared allocation comparison. It prevents an
unverified seal, provenance or accounting assumption from becoming an economic
conclusion. The change applies across regimes and changes no live rule.

**Alternatives:** reusing the historical writer by relabeling its output would
misstate provenance and funding clocks; keeping only summary results would lose
the evidence needed to reconcile accounts. Reusing the existing account engine
with explicit captured inputs preserves prior qualified arithmetic.

**Expected upside:** inspectable, reproducible evidence for a later allocation
decision. No demonstrated return improvement is attributed to these repairs.

**Next investigation:** complete the authenticated source-to-native-provider and
canonical calendar assembly, validate its exact knowledge-time selection policy
against registered source-clock controls, and qualify actual provider outputs
and full reporting across all 24 cells. The synthetic matrix is not a substitute.

economic reporting must be validated before using sealed study outcomes.

missing input windows, provider/canonical disagreement, changed frozen economics,

lost terminal exposure or archive disagreement reopens the relevant claim.
