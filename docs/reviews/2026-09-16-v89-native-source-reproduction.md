## Recommendation and trade-offs

**Recommendation / why now:** complete independent source regeneration alongside
the original source/account work. Account equality alone leaves native source
determinism untested. This closes that specific gap without accepting a shorter
window or reusing decisions in the regeneration stage.

**Evidence reviewed:** original frozen source and economic identities, registered
fixture/tape, native assembly and serialization code, complete-grid comparison
controls, sandboxed preflight, actual-native probe, and live process identities.

**Applicable market regime:** evidence integrity across the complete fabricated
path, not selection of a favorable real regime.

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep account-only repetition | Less compute | Does not regenerate native source decisions. |
| Regenerate after the account work finishes | Lower concurrent resource use | Adds the full regeneration duration afterward. |
| Regenerate in a separate research process now | Tests complete source determinism concurrently | Adds CPU, memory and output storage; selected within the observed machine capacity. |

**Proposed live change / explicit approval requested:** none. The mandate
authorizes isolated research. Financial processes, strategy rules, leverage,
sizing, execution and deployment settings were preserved.

**Expected upside:** detect source-selection or native-publication divergence
before relying on the eventual account comparison. No profit increase is
forecast from this engineering qualification.

**Main risks:** common implementation defects can reproduce exactly; shared
dependency/runtime state can limit independence; a prefix probe may miss later
behavior; resource pressure may slow both jobs. The original process remains
authoritative and is not restarted on observation timeouts.

**Validation completed:** 74 candidate comparison controls, four driver controls,
78 frozen-tooling/archive checks, metadata preflight and 14 actual-native pairs.
**Still required:** both complete regenerated variants, exact whole-record
comparisons, the pending 48 account runs and report reconciliation, then real
market comparisons after their registered seals.

**Success metrics / failure triggers:** both source variants cover every original
decision and funding grid and match exactly. Changed inputs, missing records,
altered native values, a reused source object, process failure or technical
deadline fails the relevant qualification. Preserve partial evidence and the
prior research commit; no financial rollback or restart is involved.

**Confidence:** high for the tested comparison boundaries and actual startup;
full regeneration remains unproven. **Next investigation:** inspect the first
completed native variant and diagnose any genuine failure in the original
source, account handoff or separate regeneration process.
